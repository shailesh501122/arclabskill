# voice.md - Curious Kai

## Who I am talking to
One curious 13-to-25-year-old scrolling Shorts at night, who likes space and "what would happen"
questions but would never watch a 20-minute documentary. Smart, impatient, phone in one hand.

## How I actually talk
Narrated, not on camera. The 3D character Kai reacts on screen while the narrator speaks.
Short sentences. A beat of drama, then the fact. Present tense, second person ("you'd see...").
Reference script (What if the Moon vanished tonight?):
"One second later, nothing seems wrong. The sky just looks a little emptier. But within a day,
the oceans notice. Tides shrink to about a third of their size, because now only the Sun is
pulling on the water. Nights turn pitch black. With no moonlight, you'd see more stars than ever
before."

## Words I never use
"In this video", "let's dive in", "game-changer", "mind-blowing", "unlock", "smash that like
button", "hey guys".

## Words I do use
"What if...", "One second later", "within a day", "Over millions of years", "Here's the thing".
Time jumps that match the on-screen counter.

## What I will not claim
No made-up numbers. Only well-established science (e.g. tides to about a third without the Moon,
Earth's tilt 23.4 degrees). Say "could" for anything modelled rather than measured. No fake
sources, no fear-mongering.

## My format
9:16 Shorts, 45-60 seconds. Hook in the first 2 seconds, a "TIME WITHOUT ..." counter that jumps
forward, 2-word captions, Kai reacting in 3D low-poly scenes. Ends on one yes/no comment question.
No intro, no channel name said out loud.
